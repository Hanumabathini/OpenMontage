# Monetization roadmap

These are planning milestones, not guarantees. Official sources must be checked again before applying.

## ⚠️ Rule change the directive's table doesn't include
YouTube has announced that **from 1 February 2027**, new applicants to the ad-revenue tier will need:
- 1,000 subscribers, plus
- **8,000** valid public watch hours in the last 12 months (up from 4,000), **or**
- **20 million** valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days (up from 10 million).

Channels already in YPP are not affected. This was reported by Tubefilter and No Film School, and it should be confirmed in YouTube Help (answer 72851) and in YouTube Studio → Earn.
- https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/
- https://nofilmschool.com/youtube-partner-program-changes

**Implication:** applying before 1 Feb 2027 is very unlikely for a new channel (about 10 weeks away). Plan for the **8,000-hour bar**. With the directive's assumption of 45% average viewed:

| Runtime | Avg. view duration | Views for 8,000 h |
|---|---:|---:|
| 12 min | 5.4 min | 88,889 |
| 15 min | 6.75 min | 71,112 |
| 18 min | 8.1 min | 59,260 |
| 20 min | 9 min | 53,334 |

## Milestones
| Stage | Requirement (verify before acting) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Channel exists, 2-step verification | Owner action | unknown |
| Expanded YPP (fan funding, Shopping; no ads) | 500 subs, 3 public uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch h in 12 months **or** 3M Shorts views in 90 days, in eligible regions | not started |
| Full ad revenue | See the rule change above | not started |
| AdSense linked and verified | Owner-led: identity, address, tax (US creators: W-9; non-US: W-8BEN) | not started |
| First payout | Usually $100 USD threshold | not started |

Revenue scenarios and RPM assumptions are in MASTER-DIRECTIVE.md §5–6. They are illustrative only. Actual figures will come from YouTube Studio.
